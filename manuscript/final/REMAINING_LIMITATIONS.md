# Remaining limitations

- Learners, outcomes, profile parameters, and mastery effects are synthetic and heuristic.
- The results do not establish causal learning gains, classroom effectiveness, or general educational superiority.
- The accepted experiment covers the NCERT Class 5 Mathematics pack, version 1.0.0.
- Five independent seeds limit confidence-interval resolution even with 10,000 bootstrap resamples.
- Full RAPID-Learn had lower synthetic normalized gain than Static and the BKT baselines.
- Resource awareness showed no established gain or resource-utility advantage in the matched comparison.
- Cached-path selection was rare, so real offline usefulness remains untested.
- The optional logistic-regression predictor was poorly calibrated and showed no established incremental benefit.
- Curriculum and misconception metadata are prototype-curated.
- Latency and resource measurements depend on hardware, scheduling, and runtime conditions.
- No classroom study or real-learner evaluation has been conducted.
- Bibliography publication metadata and the second-author identity remain unresolved.
